## The short version

- **Role:** Research, identity, illustration, copy, UI and full website build. Header illustrations by Kika Fuenzalida.
- **Problem:** A therapist’s outdated website felt clinical and did little to help people take the first step.
- **Decision:** A warm, mobile-first experience, abstract illustrations and direct contact options.
- **Result:** A responsive Eleventy site explaining the therapist’s services and making WhatsApp and contact details easy to reach. No conversion result was measured here.

<summary>Decisions, process and lessons</summary>

### Design for the person considering therapy

Research pointed to stigma and a cold, clinical perception of therapy. I focused the site on the therapist as a person and used abstract shapes to represent services without prescribing an age, gender or background.

<img width="2172" height="1214" src="{{ '/images/case-studies/carlos-caballero/blob-final.png' | url }}" alt="Five abstract illustrations representing individual, couple, child, teen and family therapy" loading="lazy"/>

### Keep the build simple

I built the site in Eleventy, using Markdown, Nunjucks and a modular Sass library. SVG illustrations stay crisp across screen sizes. Mobile contact options include WhatsApp and a downloadable vCard.

### What I would revisit

A spoken introduction could build on the trust established with radio listeners. Online booking was another opportunity: people visited outside normal calling hours, but the original site still relied on direct contact.
