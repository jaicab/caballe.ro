# A designer who likes making things work.

I’m Jaime Caballero, a design engineer based in London. I design systems, implement and extend existing ones, and build tools with AI. I like being close enough to the code to make the details work.

I’ve designed card experiences at **Wise**, commerce tools at **Moteefe**, booking experiences at **eola**, and moderation products at **Checkstep**. Since December 2023, I’ve also worked on freelance development for **Glass Coffee**, including its storefront and custom internal tools.

AI helps me build and iterate faster. The part I enjoy is turning its output into something people can understand, edit and use.

I have a **BS in Computer Science**, a longstanding love of typography, and a habit of opening the browser to work an idea through. Away from the screen: coffee, salsa and cooking for people.

## Working together

I’m available 1–3 days a week for design systems, frontend implementation and AI-assisted product work. I can join an existing team or take a focused problem from design through implementation.
