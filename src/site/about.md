# A designer who likes making things work.

I’m Jaime Caballero, a London-based senior product designer and frontend developer. I work across research, interaction design and frontend development — from understanding a problem to putting a working tool in someone’s hands.

I’ve designed card experiences at **Wise**, commerce tools at **Moteefe**, booking experiences at **eola**, and moderation products at **Checkstep**. Since December 2023, I’ve also worked on freelance development for **Glass Coffee**, including its storefront and custom internal tools.

I use AI to help build and iterate, with the same care for the interface, accessibility and details that make a product useful. My work combines custom UI and orchestration with established libraries; the technology serves the workflow.

I have a **BS in Computer Science**, a longstanding love of typography, and a habit of opening the browser to work an idea through. Away from the screen: coffee, salsa and cooking for people.

## Working together

I’m looking for ongoing part-time product design and design engineering work, ideally Monday–Wednesday. I’m comfortable joining an existing team or taking a focused problem from design through implementation.
